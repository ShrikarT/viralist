# Viralist

Viralist rehearses how a synthetic audience might respond to a product demo. Its outputs support editing decisions, not forecasts of real viewers.

## Language

**Simulated audience**: A cast of synthetic viewer instances whose reactions are generated under stated assumptions.
_Avoid_: real viewers, representative audience

**Archetype**: A documented behavioral pattern used to create simulated viewers; its grounding may be researched or explicitly hypothetical.
_Avoid_: validated persona, calibrated person

**Persona**: One simulated viewer with an archetype, viewing context and evolving impression.

**Viewing segment**: A bounded chronological portion of a video shown at one step; it may be an editorial scene or a subdivision of a long scene.

**Bail-out**: A persona's final decision to stop watching; it sees no later content.

**Director's Note**: A timestamped editing suggestion backed by a detected condition and inspectable simulated evidence.

**Simulation range**: Spread across stated resamples or parameter scenarios; it does not measure real-world prediction accuracy.
_Avoid_: confidence in real reach, accuracy interval

**Reach scenario**: A hypothetical propagation outcome under exposed assumptions, separate from the number of persona calls actually performed.

**Paired comparison**: Two cuts evaluated using the same cast and declared simulation settings, with uncertainty and incompatibilities shown.

**Grounding card**: Sources, assumptions, biases and last review date for an archetype; it makes no empirical fidelity claim without supporting evidence.

**Replay**: Previously recorded events rendered again, visibly distinguished from fresh inference.
